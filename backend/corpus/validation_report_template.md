# Corpus v2: IPC detector and split validation report

Generated by `backend/corpus/validation.py score` (seed $seed).

## Pre-registered labels, rule and thresholds (fixed before labelling)

- **Primary gold label (strict `in_scope`):** yes when the court decides or
  reasons about an in-scope IPC section (conviction, acquittal, sentence, its
  elements, murder vs culpable homicide, private defence, framing or quashing
  of the charge).
- **Secondary gold label (loose):** strict yes, or `section_as_charge` yes
  (the section is the charge, or is mentioned as the charge, but the court
  does not decide it). Reported alongside; never used for acceptance.
- **Primary inclusion rule:** a document is in scope if some in-scope section
  (IPC 96–106, 299–304) has at least 2 accepted body mentions, or a headnote
  mention plus at least 1 explicit body mention.
- **Sensitivity analysis only:** the looser variant (headnote mention plus any
  accepted body mention) is reported below on the same 55 documents. It is
  never used to choose the rule.
- **Acceptance:** Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70 on the strict `in_scope` label, weighted
  inclusion precision of the primary rule against the strict label ≥ 0.90,
  split accuracy ≥ 0.90. κ on `section_as_charge` is reported, not a criterion.
- **If a threshold is missed:** the rule is changed and validated on a fresh
  55 documents; these 55 then count as development documents.

## Sample

The 55 documents are drawn from five disjoint strata, so the sample is **not
natural prevalence**. Per-stratum figures are primary; pooled figures are
weighted by stratum population (N_s / n_s). Unweighted pooled figures are
shown for reference only and are not population estimates.

- Stratum populations (N_s): $population
- Quotas (n_s): $quotas
- Strata (disjoint, assigned in this order): weak_split (any split other than
  both markers or jurisdiction+heading), collision (any 96–106 mention),
  rejected_flagged, implicit_only, explicit.
- Excluded: development documents (`corpus/dev_docs.json`), the 5
  calibration documents, and PDFs that failed (failed, missing,
  extraction_failed in the manifest).
- The headnote/body split rules were frozen before this sample was drawn.
- Documents labelled "unsure" by adjudication, excluded from strict-label
  detector metrics: $unsure (loose label: $secondary_unsure)
