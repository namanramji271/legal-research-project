# Corpus v2: IPC detector and split validation report

Generated by `backend/corpus/validation.py score` (seed $seed).

## Pre-registered rule and thresholds (fixed before labelling)

- **Primary inclusion rule:** a document is in scope if some in-scope section
  (IPC 96–106, 299–304) has at least 2 accepted body mentions, or a headnote
  mention plus at least 1 explicit body mention.
- **Sensitivity analysis only:** the looser variant (headnote mention plus any
  accepted body mention) is reported below on the same 50 documents. It is
  never used to choose the rule.
- **Acceptance:** Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70 (document level), weighted inclusion
  precision of the primary rule ≥ 0.90, split accuracy ≥ 0.90.
- **If a threshold is missed:** the rule is changed and validated on a fresh
  50 documents; these 50 then count as development documents.

## Sample

The 50 documents are drawn from five disjoint strata, so the sample is **not
natural prevalence**. Per-stratum figures are primary; pooled figures are
weighted by stratum population (N_s / n_s). Unweighted pooled figures are
shown for reference only and are not population estimates.

- Stratum populations (N_s): $population

- Strata (disjoint, assigned in this order): weak_split (any split other than

- Excluded: development documents (`corpus/dev_docs.json`), the 5
  calibration documents, and PDFs that failed (failed, missing,
