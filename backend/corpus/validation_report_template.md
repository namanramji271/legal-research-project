# Corpus v2: IPC detector and split validation report

Generated by `backend/corpus/validation.py score` (seed $seed).

## Pre-registered labels, rule and thresholds (fixed before labelling)

- **Primary gold label (strict `in_scope`):** yes when the court decides or
  reasons about an in-scope IPC section (conviction, acquittal, sentence, its
  elements, murder vs culpable homicide, private defence, framing or quashing
  of the charge).
- **Secondary gold label (loose):** strict yes, or `section_as_charge` yes
  (the section is the charge, or is mentioned as the charge, but the court
  does not decide it). Reported alongside; never used for acceptance.
- **Primary inclusion rule:** a document is in scope if some in-scope section
  (IPC 96–106, 299–304) has at least 2 accepted body mentions, or a headnote
  mention plus at least 1 explicit body mention.
- **Sensitivity analysis only:** the looser variant (headnote mention plus any
  accepted body mention) is reported below on the same 55 documents. It is
  never used to choose the rule.
- **Annotation design ("Option B"):** annotator 1 is an LLM, one fresh
  session per document with no tools or files, given only the guideline and
  that document's title, citation, headnote, body opening and full body; it
  labels all 55 and quotes its decisive sentence. Annotator 2 is a legally
  trained human who labels, blind, a seeded stratified subset of 25 (5 per
  stratum) plus the 5 calibration documents (calibration is discussed
  jointly and not scored).
- **Agreement and gold:** κ on strict `in_scope` between the LLM and the blind
  human labels on the 25. If κ ≥ 0.70 the LLM labels are gold for the other
  30; otherwise those 30 are not used until a human labels them. On the 25,
  disagreements are adjudicated by the human after reading the LLM's reason;
  the human label wins and every disagreement is logged.
- **Acceptance:** κ ≥ 0.70 (LLM vs human, strict `in_scope`, 25 overlap),
  weighted inclusion precision of the primary rule against the strict label
  ≥ 0.90, split accuracy ≥ 0.90. κ on `section_as_charge` is reported, not a
  criterion. Detector and split metrics are computed on all 55 adjudicated
  labels, and on the 25 human labels alone as a sensitivity analysis.
- **If a detector threshold is missed:** the rule is changed and validated on
  a fresh 55 documents; these 55 then count as development documents. If
  only κ is missed, the verdict is INCOMPLETE until a human labels the 30.

## Uncertainty

Every figure is shown as `point [95% interval]`. Per-stratum proportions
(and unweighted pooled and section-level figures) use the Wilson score
interval. The weighted pooled precision/recall and the split accuracy use a
stratified percentile bootstrap ($resamples resamples, seed $seed): each
stratum is resampled with replacement at its own size, so the N_s / n_s
weights stay fixed.

**The pooled weighted estimate rests on few labels per stratum**
($min_quota–$max_quota documents each in the main analysis, $human_per_stratum
in the 25-only sensitivity analysis), and each stratum's weight is its
population share, so a single label in a heavily weighted stratum moves the
pooled figure a lot. Read the intervals before the point estimates. A
bootstrap interval collapses to a point when no errors of that kind were
observed in the sample (e.g. recall `1.0 [1.0, 1.0]` with no false
negatives); that does not mean there is no uncertainty, so read the
per-stratum Wilson intervals instead. Acceptance is still judged on the
point estimates, as pre-registered; the intervals are not an acceptance
criterion.
